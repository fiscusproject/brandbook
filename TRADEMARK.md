# Fiscus Trademark & Brand Policy

*Version 1.0 — July 2026*

The **Fiscus** name, the Fiscus seal mark (the navy-and-gold woven-basket seal), the
**FISCUS** wordmark, and the lockups, banners, and derivative assets in this repository
(together, **"the Fiscus marks"**) are trademarks of the **Fiscus project** and its
maintainers, whether or not registered. This document explains what you may do with
them without asking, and when you need permission first.

The intent is the usual open-source balance: we *want* you to talk about Fiscus, write
about it, and tell people your product integrates with it — while making sure nobody is
misled about what is, and is not, the official project. Fiscus handles fiscal reporting,
where being misled about "official" has real consequences, so we take the second half
seriously.

## The marks are not under the code license

Fiscus source code is published under an open-source license in its own repositories.
**That license covers code and grants no rights to the Fiscus marks.** Likewise, this
repository being publicly visible does not place its contents under an open-source
license. All rights in the marks are reserved except as granted below.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project.** Use the name "Fiscus" in articles, blog posts, talks,
  papers, documentation, and social media to refer truthfully to the project
  (nominative use).
- **State factual relationships.** Say your product *"works with Fiscus"*, *"integrates
  with Fiscus"*, or *"is built on Fiscus"* — provided it is true.
- **Show the unmodified seal or lockup** when referring or linking to the official
  project: in integration listings, architecture diagrams, documentation, slides, and
  news coverage. Follow the design rules in [README.md](README.md) (clear space, no
  recoloring, no distortion).
- **Name compatible software descriptively.** Client libraries, plugins, and connectors
  may use the name descriptively — e.g. `fiscus-client-python` or
  `pos-fiscus-connector` — as long as the name does not suggest the package is official
  and its README states it is a third-party project.
- **Redistribute the asset files in this repository, unmodified,** for any of the
  purposes above.

## You need permission first

- Using the Fiscus marks in a company name, product name, service name, domain name, or
  app-store listing where Fiscus is the primary identity rather than a descriptive
  reference.
- Creating modified or derived versions of the seal — recoloring, redrawing, combining
  it with other marks, or using the weave motif as part of another logo.
- Putting the marks on merchandise, whether or not sold.
- Any use that implies sponsorship, endorsement, or official status that has not been
  agreed.

## Forks and modified distributions

You may truthfully describe a fork's origin ("a fork of Fiscus", "based on Fiscus").
However, a **modified** distribution must ship under its own name and its own logo: it
must not use the Fiscus seal, wordmark, or a confusingly similar name, and must not
present itself as the official project or as endorsed by it. Unmodified redistributions
(e.g. packaging for a distro) may keep the name and mark, clearly attributed.

## Never allowed

Using the marks to deceive: impersonating the project or its maintainers, phishing,
misrepresenting compliance status of software, or suggesting a tax authority's
endorsement. No provision of this policy permits such use.

## Questions and permission requests

If your use isn't clearly covered above, ask — the default answer to reasonable
requests is yes. Contact: **info@fiscusproject.eu** (or open an issue in this
repository for non-confidential questions).
